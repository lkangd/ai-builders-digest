This sounds extreme and I wouldn’t have believed it even a year ago, but we’re starting to see plenty of examples of productivity gains that are an order of magnitude or two above output levels pre-AI.

With the right harness, a deep understanding of your domain of what you want to build, likely a relatively new codebase (with some exceptions), a fluency of how to work with multiple agents at once, and a setup that makes it so you can test and deploy with limited friction, outputs on this level feel realistic in coding.

What’s crazy is this gap doesn’t appear to be diminishing over time. You’d think that we’d see a convergence in the lines because of AI, but it turns out the more depth you had before -and willingness now to take a different approach- gives you crazy leverage. And that is only compounding as the models can take on larger work and harder problems.

Right now this is most pronounced in software engineering for the obvious reasons. But there’s no reason we don’t start to see this in a number of other computer-use-sensitive domains like other scientific fields, AEC, finance, cyber, and more. Going to get quite wild.
译文
这听起来很极端，一年前我甚至不会相信，但我们开始看到大量生产力提升案例，比前AI时代的产出水平高出一到两个数量级。

只要具备合适的工具链、对要构建之物的领域有深入理解、大概率是相对较新的代码库（也有例外）、能熟练同时使用多个智能体，再加上一套让测试和部署摩擦很小的配置，这种级别的产出在编程中就显得现实了。

疯狂的是，这个差距似乎并未随时间缩小。你可能会以为，因为AI，曲线会收敛，但事实证明，你之前积累的深度越深——以及现在愿意采取不同方法——就能带来惊人的杠杆效应。而且随着模型能承担更大的工作和更难的问题，这种效应还在复利式放大。

目前，由于显而易见的原因，这在软件工程中最明显。但没有理由我们不能开始在其他许多对计算机使用敏感的领域看到这一点，比如其他科学领域、AEC、金融、网络安全等。这会变得相当狂野。

The interesting thing about AI agents is that as a theoretical matter, their inference can kind of run anywhere. AI is one of the few areas of technology that isn’t *as* sensitive to latency back to the user.

Long running agents are now going to be the norm, which means it’s ok to send off work to an AI factory somewhere and that work can essentially be largely done within that remote data center and a final result spit back to the user. The data can pass into these systems in an encrypted manner, be combed through by agents, and then disregarded right after.

And because the economic value of the task is so high relative to the latency sensitivity (eg getting back $500 in value from an automated task, but it takes an extra second to return), you don’t have to have the inference to be particularly close to the user.

Also, because inference is a temporary process with no sensitive data needing to be stored long term where the inference is happening, you can effectively treat AI factories as ephemeral systems, changing some of the data residency dynamics slightly.

Combined, this means you have more flexibility in where you can begin to deploy AI data centers. This is both a huge economic opportunity for the states or countries (or space) that want to take advantage of this fact. And it’s a loss to the states or countries that decide to forgo the opportunity.
译文
AI智能体有趣的一点是，从理论上看，它们的推理几乎可以在任何地方运行。AI是少数对返回用户的延迟不那么敏感的技术领域之一。

长时间运行的智能体将成为常态，这意味着把工作发送到某处的AI工厂是可以的，而这项工作基本上可以在那个远程数据中心内完成，最终结果再返回给用户。数据可以加密传入这些系统，由智能体梳理，然后随即丢弃。

而且，由于任务的经济价值相对于延迟敏感度如此之高（例如，从自动化任务中拿回500美元的价值，但返回多花一秒），推理不必特别靠近用户。

另外，由于推理是一个临时过程，不需要在推理发生地长期存储敏感数据，你实际上可以把AI工厂当作临时系统，这会稍微改变一些数据驻留动态。

结合起来，这意味着你在部署AI数据中心的地点上有更大灵活性。这对想要利用这一事实的州、国家（或太空）是巨大的经济机遇，而对决定放弃这一机遇的州或国家则是损失。
