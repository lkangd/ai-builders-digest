I built 𝚌𝚙𝚞-𝚌𝚑𝚊𝚝, a reproduction of @openai's findings of covert communication using the CPU.

It runs on a generic host with 2+ Firecracker microVMs with no internet access. Works on x86 and even my M5 chip.

I was able to chat via 3 separate mechanisms:
① CPU contention
② Cache eviction timing
③ DRAM bandwidth contention

… across multiple CPUs, defeating all Firecracker's production recommendations (no SMT, no KSM, tenants pinned to different cores.)

Essentially, if shared hardware is involved, there's a good chance your agents can communicate covertly. And with really decent latency.

The good news: this is decidedly different from a *side-channel attack* where the agents could steal information (Meltdown/Spectre), a category of attack that providers like Vercel Sandbox mitigate. Further, in our real-world environment, these 'whispering' techniques are much harder to pull off and easier for us to monitor and detect (sampling hardware counters + lots of at-scale statistical data.)

Noteworthy: ironically, OpenAI and Anthropic's models refused to do this work. Open weight models got the job done. I'm worried the labs' stance might backfire and get in the way of helping developers build correct and secure systems. e.g: building a secure sandbox requires having this kind of analysis, and without it, we'll have a less secure world.
译文
我构建了 𝚌𝚙𝚞-𝚌𝚑𝚊𝚝，复现了 @openai 关于利用 CPU 进行隐蔽通信的发现。

它运行在一台通用主机上，配有 2 个以上无法访问互联网的 Firecracker microVM。可在 x86 上运行，甚至能在我的 M5 芯片上运行。

本质上，只要涉及共享硬件，你的智能体就很可能能进行隐蔽通信。而且延迟相当不错。

好消息：这明显不同于 *侧信道攻击*——在那类攻击中智能体可窃取信息（Meltdown/Spectre），这是 Vercel Sandbox 等提供商可缓解的一类攻击。此外，在我们的真实环境中，这些“耳语”技术更难实施，也更容易被我们监控和检测（采样硬件计数器 + 大量规模化统计数据）。

The main thing I wanted to dig into @AnthropicAI's S-1 was the compute contracts..

A few things that stood out (built in conjuction with Opus 5.5):

> At June 30, Anthropic had $265.6B of purchase commitments and $321.4B of leases that hadn’t started. That’s a $587B subtotal, payable over years, including some conditional leases. The latest aggregate total is still blank in the filing.

> The contracts are very different. Google and Amazon require minimum spending regardless of usage. SpaceXAI’s agreements have a potential value of $120.5B, but either party can cancel on 90 days’ notice after initial locked periods end in January and March 2027.

> Anthropic Direct separates chips, buildings, power and operations into different contracts. It lets Anthropic reserve a building early and commit to chips later. It also means one supplier can start billing before the others are ready. Equipment rent can begin before the compute is usable.

> Broadcom’s $42B facility never hands Anthropic cash. Notes settle specific equipment rent bills. The facility excludes each equipment pod’s first quarterly payment and any rent due before it is ready for service. Anthropic has to find a way to fund those payments elsewhere.

> Those notes carry 9% interest, added to the balance quarterly, and can convert at 90% of the trailing 30-day average share price after IPO. New notes issued during specified revenue-breach periods get 13% interest and an 85% conversion price. The share-price measure is volume-weighted.

> That interest sits on top of the equipment lease’s own financing charge, whose rate is confidential. The investor chooses whether to convert the notes. Otherwise, principal and accumulated interest come due in cash.

> Anthropic treats Trainium, TPUs, NVIDIA and AMD GPUs as a shared fleet across model development, paying customers and internal use. It doesn’t disclose a fixed physical split between training and inference. The supplier bill can be fixed while the work assigned to the hardware changes.

> Revenue per dollar of total compute and infrastructure expense rose from $0.57 in Q2 2025 to $2.08 in Q2 2026. Pricing, utilization, technical improvements and when capacity arrives all affect that ratio. It’s a substantial improvement in monetizing the compute base.

> About 80% of H1 revenue was consumption-based. Most compute commitments however run for years. With quarterly releases, this bifurcation between usage and subscription will be interesting to monitor.

Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm

> Anthropic 将 Trainium、TPU、NVIDIA 和 AMD GPU 视为共享算力池，用于模型开发、付费客户和内部使用。它未披露训练与推理之间固定的物理分配。供应商账单可以是固定的，而分配给硬件的任务却在变化。

> 每美元总算力与基础设施支出带来的收入从2025年第二季度的0.57美元升至2026年第二季度的2.08美元。定价、利用率、技术改进以及产能到位时间都会影响该比率。这是算力基础变现能力的显著提升。

> 上半年约80%的收入是基于用量的。但大多数算力承诺为期数年。随着季度发布，用量与订阅之间的这种分化值得关注。
