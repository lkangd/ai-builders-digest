Every AI product should be working towards abstracting model choice away from users.

In any product, the intelligence is the underlying engine - all the user cares about is that the job gets done.

Model picking is too much cognitive load for the user.

There have been multiple attempts to abstract model choice away from users. I think we’re finally at the point where this can work well.

It takes three things:

1/ Deep understanding of the model frontier across different weight classes for your particular use cases.

2/ Great evals for your workflows, so you know which models work best where.

3/ A team with the talent and AI-native operating model to continuously adapt as the frontier moves.

Having worked with the Lovable team, I can say they have all three.
译文
每一款 AI 产品都应致力于让用户无需自行选择模型。

在任何产品中，智能都是底层引擎——用户只关心任务能否完成。

让用户挑模型，认知负担太重。

过去已有多番尝试让用户摆脱模型选择。我认为我们终于到了能让它真正落地的阶段。

这需要三件事：

1/ 针对你的具体用例，深入理解不同参数级别模型的能力前沿。

2/ 为工作流建立出色的评测体系，清楚哪些模型在何处表现最佳。

3/ 一支具备能力、采用 AI 原生运营模式的团队，能随前沿演进持续调整。

与 Lovable 团队合作过，我可以说三者他们都具备。
