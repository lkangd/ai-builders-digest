Andrej Karpathy@karpathy
 X Update
2026-11-26 10:31
I asked Claude to populate a Civilization-style game map with real data from the US. Opus 5.5 downloaded and parse a ton of public data and 7 hours and 45K lines of code later... it works :)

- 31 real data sources including Census, BEA, BLS, EIA, NOAA, USGS, DoD, NPS, FAA, FEMA, CDC...
- Real elevation, land cover, GDP, jobs, power plants, mines, ports, airports, bases, parks, universities, freight flows, disasters; all reconciled onto one 25 km hex grid.
- The data is converted into Civilization language, e.g. Gold yield is GDP. A city's districts are its real industry specializations: San Jose gets a Campus, Detroit is "Motor city". Its citizens are its real workforce: 1 in 4 San Jose workers are engineers, coders or scientists, against 1 in 14 nationally. Wonders are chosen by published rules, such as national parks by visitor numbers and universities by R&D spending, etc etc.
- 19 lenses (income, degrees, faith, disasters…), a City View for 524 metros, and nine advisors plus an Explorer. The Explorer turns any column for any of 3,100+ counties into a table and scatter plot, linked to the map.
- Claude decided to skip a few states and territories sry
- Built using Claude Code desktop app, Opus 5.5 on medium and turning on ultracode.

It's deployed here: https://karpathy.ai/usaciv/ and the "?" has the source link too. I don't exactly know where I was going with this but it's really fun to browse and play with. Possibly it can be expanded or built into a game. I only have half of my weekly Claude quote left though so someone else will have to take over :D. Claude also made a trailer:

AI native teams will eventually force the surrounding organization to become AI native too.

Take software development. coding velocity is way up thanks to AI, while actual shipped products and business impact are unchanged in most companies. That’s cos the surrounding system of experimentation tooling, evaluation, launch processes, and gtm are outdated.

It’s like sticking a Ferrari engine in a sedan and racing it on Indian roads. The engine can rev faster but the car speed is limited by its system and environment.

AI native companies were built ground up for this new way of building and shipping. They’re also able to shed suboptimal processes faster.

Market forces will quickly force all companies to adapt or perish.

If you’re solving this for your company: map out your product lifecycle and rebuild it ground up - tooling, team structure, gtm, everything.
译文
AI native 团队最终会倒逼周围的组织也变成 AI native。

就拿软件开发来说。多亏了 AI，写代码的速度已经快了很多，但在大多数公司里，真正上线的产品和业务影响并没有变化。因为周围那套实验工具、评估、发布流程和 GTM，都已经过时了。

这就像把一颗 Ferrari 引擎塞进一辆轿车，然后开到印度的路上比赛。引擎可以转得更快，但车速受限于整车系统和环境。

AI native 公司是从零按这种新的构建和交付方式建起来的。它们也能更快地甩掉不够优的流程。

市场力量会迅速迫使所有公司要么适应，要么淘汰。

如果你正在为自己的公司解决这个问题：把产品生命周期完整画出来，再从零重建，工具、团队结构、GTM，所有环节都要动。

Coding gives you a window into what the future will look like for a large amount of knowledge work.

The first step for most jobs is that we go from doing most of the research, typing, and value creation manually (even if it’s using digital tools) to soon coordinating the work that agents are doing.

Next, as this step itself becomes redundant or slows down what agents are capable of, we will be giving agents goals to optimize against and they’ll go off on and increasingly solve larger problems for us.

This may at first blush look like something whose impact gets stuck with coding, but actually a large percentage of knowledge work may eventually just converge with this form factor due to the massive productivity gains that can be generated. Agents will use computers, leverage our existing tools or write new ones, and write code on the fly to solve larger swaths of knowledge work.

So even if a task today doesn’t seem to be “coding” directly, the task may eventually fold into being coding adjacent. Manufacturing, architecture, life sciences, material sciences, cyber, and on and on.

One big hurdle in the way ultimately will be that much of this work remains non-verifiable, so humans will be even more in the loop than they are in coding. But the part that they can hand off will just get bigger and bigger over time as the models are more capable.

So in short, pay attention to coding and you’ll get a sense for where work is going.

我是一个信念很强、极度有热情、能力很强的 operator，但我也可能会情绪化、显得凌乱，并且急于分享自己的想法，而不是先收集团队的反馈。在最好的状态下，我以好奇心来带领；在最差的状态下，我不会这样。

@danshipper 给了一个很好的主意，让我可以训练自己成为想成为的那种领导者。他让我去找我的 @OpenAI Dot（Milo）帮忙。它本来就已经在阅读我所有的 Slack 消息。于是我把它连接到 @usemonologue（我在那里记录所有会议），并让它也审阅这些内容。

我的指示很简单：我希望沟通起来更有高管风范。我希望以好奇心来带领。而且我希望你尽可能给我接近实时的反馈。

我很快发现发生了两件事：
1. 还不等 Milo 提醒，我就已经在以好奇心来带领了。我知道有什么东西在跟踪我的一举一动，所以我会主动按自己知道应该做的方式去行动。
2. 当我失手时，Milo 会向我指出；有好几次，我迅速改了消息，或者澄清了自己在会上说过的话。我是在实时改掉坏习惯。

短短几周，我真的觉得自己的领导能力和沟通都倍增了。我想我的团队也看得到。而最重要的是，我感觉更平静了。我能更从容地应对业务中的起伏。

从来没有比现在更好的时机，去做最好的自己和最好的领导者。沟通是工作中最重要的事，而现在，只要你愿意告诉它你想达成什么，并真正按反馈去行动，你就可以拥有一位始终在线的教练。

这是我用的 skill。把它放进你选择的个人 agent，并连接到你的 Slack/Teams 和会议记录。你不会后悔的！
