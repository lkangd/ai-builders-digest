The big bitter lesson question in the applied AI layer is that as models get better and better, what is the best way to diffuse that intelligence into an enterprise.

Ironically, I’d argue that as models are getting more capable, and can handle longer running tasks and better tool use, the ability to differentiate on a domain by domain basis goes up instead of down.

When models were quite limited in what they could do, ironically all you could be literally was a “wrapper” because you were effectively delivering tokens back to the user with a custom prompt or some limited RAG.

But in a world of agents using tools, tasks that run on for hours or days, workflows that kick off agents, agent swarms that need to be coordinated against a goal, data sources that are domain specific, model routing that requires domain-specific routing, implementation that requires real change management of the workflow instead of “just type into this chat box”, it’s very very possible that specialization of these systems goes up more.

Obviously this doesn’t apply to everything - the closer you are to the labs core use-cases the tougher this play gets, but I’d argue we’re going to see autonomous systems for a wide range of job functions that weren’t practical to build out before. We’ll see this in security, life sciences, healthcare, finance, manufacturing, and many other fields. Either way going to be a fun and wild couple of years.
译文
应用 AI 层关于“苦涩教训”的大问题是：随着模型越来越好，把这种智能扩散到企业里的最佳方式是什么？

讽刺的是，我认为随着模型能力更强、能处理运行更久的任务并更好地使用工具，按领域实现差异化的能力反而上升，而不是下降。

当模型能力还相当有限时，讽刺的是你能做的实际上就是个“wrapper”，因为你本质上只是靠自定义提示或有限的 RAG 把 token 返回给用户。

但在一个由使用工具的智能体、持续数小时或数天的任务、触发智能体的工作流、需要围绕目标协调的智能体集群、领域特定的数据源、需要领域特定路由的模型路由，以及需要真正对工作流进行变革管理而非“只在聊天框里输入”的实施所构成的世界里，这些系统的专业化程度很可能会更高。

显然这并非适用于一切——你越接近实验室的核心用例，这种打法就越难，但我认为我们将看到面向广泛职能的自主系统，而这些系统以前并不实际可建。我们会在安全、生命科学、医疗、金融、制造以及许多其他领域看到这一点。无论如何，接下来几年会很有趣也很疯狂。

A few weeks ago, I was ranting about multiplayer AI experiences and that's when @ekuyda shared an early preview of Wabi 2.0..

Truth be told, I was one of the early users of Wabi 1.0 and while the design was gorgeous, it frankly never "stuck" with me.

But, in moments of downloading this edition, I was immediately hooked.

It just gets the "paradigm" right imo. There are two types of personal agents today: 1) Singleplayer iMessage or Muse types - while useful it's hard to compartmentalize them when many things are going on 2) Build your own multiple chat Hermes agents - while that's useful and can share context, they live forever

Wabi has this great paradigm of orchestrator (which is the main agent) and topic specific episodic chats that are for a single purpose. You can share these individual chats with folks (for example the school one with my wife). And these are generated proactively AND don't need to stay forever (which is important).

For example, for my recent NY trip, it was generated based on a flight it saw, provided help where needed, and then after the trip was done, it was archived. Same with my 2025 taxes.

My only critique so far it's TOO proactive, the apps can be better and they need to dial both of those in, but this is a wonderful product that everyone should try!

(I know it's shocking to see this in today's day and age, but this is neither paid, nor an investor, just a fan)
