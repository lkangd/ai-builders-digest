Notes From the HubSpot Refounding, Day 14 (Wed, Nov 25):

I've been spending a lot of time today *thinking* about strategy and market opportunity.

Here's what's rattling around in my brain:

Agentic Coding has been a massive use case for AI and has produced a ton of economic value for the companies that built those products. I'm not surprised, because the output of agentic coding is...code. And that code (software) can be used to do economically valuable things -- including *selling* that software and getting revenue.

The value is amplified because historically it has been hard and expensive to create software. Historically, humans have had to write code manually. Like cave people! Not any more, we have the tools so humans can be more productive and produce better software faster.

Now, time for a logical leap...

Another thing that is economically valuable and hard to create is...growth. And by growth, I mean in the business sense. Making sales/revenue/customers go up. Historically, humans have had to create things like marketing campaigns, inbound lead workflows and other "artifacts" that then helped drive revenue growth.

In the world of agentic coding, you need a great purpose-built harness + a great LLM + context (the code + special skills + design guides) and a system of record (code repo) to produce the outcome needed.

In the world of agentic growth, you need a great purpose-built harness + a great LLM + context (system of record + a second brain for the organization) in order to produce the outcome needed.

And this is what we're focused on at HubSpot with the refounding.

We're building the world's best purpose-built GTM harness which uses a mix of great LLMs + an AI-native context store (CRM database + second brain).

The HubSpot mission is to help millions grow better.

The way we do that is to help humans responsible for delivering growth to leverage AI for the painful, manual stuff. And just like agentic coding hasn't killed the software builder, agentic growth doesn't kill the need for the high-agency marketing, sales person and revenue leaders.

Just thinking out loud. What do you think?

#HubSpotRefounding

只是把想法说出来。你怎么看？

12 tips for people who posted on LinkedIn but now wanna post on X

A lot of people I know are moving their posting from LinkedIn to X. When they ask me how I grew 80k followers on X in one year, here’s what I tell them:

1. LinkedIn is a stage. X is a party. First you find your crowd, then you listen to what they're already talking about. Don't start talking until you get it

2. You can't reach the C-suite directly. LinkedIn is a professional network, so you can go straight to decision makers. X doesn't work like that. You have to get mass attention first, and then the right people notice you

3. Post way more than you think. On LinkedIn, 3-5 posts a week counts as a lot. I posted 3 times a day on X for 6 months. Some founders I follow post 5 times a day. It's not that they're on X all day. They just don't overthink it

4. Make posting a reflex. If you spend too long on a post, you won't post. Whenever a thought comes up, post it right there. No planning, no scheduling, no waiting for the right time

5. It's a numbers game. Most posts won't do well. Maybe 1 in 10 will. Maybe 1 in 50 goes viral. That's normal

6. Half your posts should be reactions. LinkedIn is mostly “proactive content”. On X, at least at the start, half of what you post should be “reactive content”: replies, quote tweets, commentary on what others already said

7. Know the difference between a reply and a quote tweet. A reply is like a LinkedIn DM. It says "notice me." A quote tweet uses someone else's content to grow your own account

8. Fix your feed first. If you wanna talk about cybersecurity and your feed is all politics, you can't join the right conversations. Pick a few big names in your niche. See who they follow and who they engage with, and follow those people too. Like, bookmark and comment. After about a week your timeline will look totally different

9. Write like you're texting an acquaintance. Polish turns people off. Everyone's allergic to perfect AI-sounding writing. And please don't use AI or bots to write your replies. People can tell, and they might block you

10. Post the stuff that's obvious to you. Make a list of the top 10 questions customers, investors and your team ask you. That's where you're the expert. What seems too obvious to talk about is exactly what people want to hear

11. Post what you just said out loud. If you say something in real life and people go "huh, never thought of it that way," post it on your way back. Don't wait, you'll forget. You don't have to block out time to think of what to say. Just say what you've already said

12. Show your face, and don't sell yet. X is a wall of text, so video stands out. I just record myself on my laptop with something on the screen. People wanna know you're a real human, not AI slop. And until you have about 1,000 followers, don't promote your company. Share real opinions first. Later, when you repost company news, always add your own take, not PR speak.

Remember: people follow people, not companies

写给以前在 LinkedIn 发帖、现在想转到 X 上发的人的 12 条建议

我认识的很多人正在把发帖阵地从 LinkedIn 转到 X。当他们问我如何在一年内在 X 上涨到 8 万粉丝时，我是这样说的：

1. LinkedIn 是舞台，X 是派对。先找到你的人群，再听他们已经在聊什么。搞明白之前先别急着开口

2. 你没法直接触达 C-suite。LinkedIn 是职业网络，所以你可以直接找到决策者。X 不是这样运作的。你得先获得大规模关注，然后对的人才会注意到你

3. 发得要比你以为的多得多。在 LinkedIn 上，一周 3 到 5 条已经算很多。我在 X 上每天发 3 条，持续了 6 个月。我关注的一些创始人每天发 5 条。不是他们整天泡在 X 上，只是他们不会想太多

4. 让发帖变成反射。如果一条帖子你花太久，你就不会发了。一有想法，当场就发。不规划，不定时，不等所谓的合适时机

5. 这是场数量游戏。大多数帖子不会有好表现。也许 10 条里有 1 条会。也许 50 条里有 1 条会爆。这很正常

6. 一半的帖子应该是反应。LinkedIn 大多是“主动内容”。在 X 上，至少一开始，你发的内容里应该有一半是“反应内容”：回复、引用推文、对别人已经说过的话发表评论

7. 分清回复和引用推文的区别。回复就像 LinkedIn 私信，意思是“注意我”。引用推文是借别人的内容来增长你自己的账号

8. 先把信息流修好。如果你想聊网络安全，但你的信息流全是政治，你就加入不了正确的对话。在你的领域里选几个大人物，看看他们关注谁、和谁互动，把那些人也关注上。点赞、收藏、评论。大约一周后，你的时间线会完全不一样

9. 写得像在给熟人发短信。过于打磨会让人反感。大家都对听起来完美的、像 AI 写的文字过敏。而且请不要用 AI 或 bots 来写回复。别人看得出来，还可能会拉黑你

10. 发那些对你来说显而易见的东西。列出客户、投资人和团队最常问你的 10 个问题。那就是你的专长所在。你觉得太显而易见、不值得说的，恰恰是人们想听的

11. 把你刚刚大声说过的话发出去。如果你在现实里说了什么，别人反应是“嗯，从没这么想过”，那就在回去的路上发出来。别等，你会忘的。你不必专门腾出时间去想该说什么，把已经说过的话说出来就行

12. 露脸，但先别卖东西。X 是一堵文字墙，所以视频会很显眼。我就是用笔记本电脑录自己，屏幕上放点东西。人们想知道你是真人，不是 AI slop。而且在大约有 1000 个粉丝之前，先别推广你的公司。先分享真实观点。之后再转发公司消息时，一定要加上你自己的看法，而不是公关稿腔。

记住：人们关注的是人，不是公司
