Introducing gdp-ts: Ghosts of Departed Proofs for TypeScript.

gdp-ts is a library, linter and AI skill for safer API design. Under this contract, sensitive functions require 'proofs' that the caller performed an authorization check.

The typechecker verifies these proofs at compile time, preventing your team and agents from shipping catastrophic security (and other kinds of) bugs.

While these patterns have existed for quite some time, especially in ecosystems like Haskell, ① human code review and ② cognitive and syntactic overhead made these solutions niche.

The situation is now inverted. Agents are writing more code than we can review, and they *thrive* in tight loops with hard constraints that would frustrate us. We see this with the rise of Rust, borrow checker, code aesthetics debate and all.

The README and examples model a real-world Vercel API product constraint: changing the password on a Project requires a proof of a certain role + a certain entitlement. Thanks to Matt Noonan and Ollie Charles for their research in this space.

https://github.com/rauchg/gdp-ts
译文
介绍 gdp-ts：TypeScript 的 Ghosts of Departed Proofs。

gdp-ts 是一个库、linter 和 AI 技能，用于更安全的 API 设计。在此契约下，敏感函数要求调用者提供已执行授权检查的“证明”。

类型检查器会在编译时验证这些证明，防止你的团队和智能体发布灾难性的安全（及其他类型）bug。

尽管这些模式已存在相当长时间，尤其在 Haskell 等生态系统中，但 ① 人工代码审查和 ② 认知与语法开销，使这些方案一直很小众。

如今情况反转了。智能体写的代码多到我们根本审不过来，而它们在带有硬性约束的紧密循环中 *如鱼得水*，这些约束会让我们感到挫败。从 Rust、borrow checker、代码美学之争等的兴起中，我们看到了这一点。

README 和示例模拟了一个真实的 Vercel API 产品约束：更改 Project 的密码需要某个角色 + 某项权益的证明。感谢 Matt Noonan 和 Ollie Charles 在该领域的研究。

https://github.com/rauchg/gdp-ts

remembering Steve

i grew up with beige boxes. then i moved to Canada and saw the iMac G4.

how can computers be so beautiful?

i couldn’t afford a Mac, so i made my PC look like one. changed the icons, the fonts, the windows. learned to make those shiny, lickable buttons. that’s how i got into interface design. i saw something i loved and wanted to make things like it.

i burned DVDs of Steve’s keynotes and studied them. watched them again and again. there was so much care in the products, and so much joy in showing people what they could do.

my first iPod was a black nano. i carried it everywhere. then the iPhone happened and changed the world.

it felt like anything was possible.

since he died, i’ve felt us getting stuck in a loop. constant refinement. better supply chains. more ways to keep people scrolling. so much talent spent on tiny improvements, so little room for something truly great and heartfelt.

the keynotes get more cinematic every year. i still miss the live demos. someone onstage playing with something they helped make. the excitement, the jokes, the things that went wrong. it felt human. it was fun.

we made computers faster. phones thinner. added more cameras. made machines smarter than us at more and more things. somehow, we’ve also become more disconnected and lonely.

we spend so much energy trying to beat humans. i wish we spent more time learning from people’s lives and making them better.

when i work, i still sometimes think: what would Steve do?

i’ll never know. i’m sad i never got to work with him. but his work reached a kid with a beige PC and changed the course of his life. i wonder how many others have a story like mine.

i’ve kept these words with me:

“the people who are crazy enough to think they can change the world, are the ones who do.”

there’s still so much worth making. things we can put our hearts into. things that make someone feel what i felt when i first saw that iMac.

thank you, Steve. 💛
译文
怀念 Steve

我从小用的就是米色机箱。后来搬到加拿大，看到了 iMac G4。

电脑怎么能这么美？

我把 Steve 主题演讲的 DVD 刻录下来，反复研究。看了一遍又一遍。产品里有那么多用心，向人们展示它们能做什么时又有那么多快乐。

我的第一台 iPod 是黑色 nano。我走到哪带到哪。后来 iPhone 出现，改变了世界。

感觉一切皆有可能。

他去世后，我觉得我们陷入了一个循环。不断打磨。更好的供应链。更多让人不停刷下去的方式。那么多才华花在微小改进上，却几乎没有空间留给真正伟大而真挚的东西。

发布会一年比一年更像电影。我还是怀念现场演示。有人站在台上，玩着自己参与打造的东西。那种兴奋、玩笑，还有出错的地方。感觉很有人情味。也很有趣。

我们让电脑更快，手机更薄，加了更多摄像头，让机器在越来越多事情上比我们更聪明。不知怎的，我们也变得更疏离、更孤独。

我们花那么多精力试图超越人类。我希望我们花更多时间去了解人们的生活，并让生活变得更好。

工作时，我有时仍会想：Steve 会怎么做？

我永远不会知道。很遗憾没能和他共事。但他的作品触达了一个用米色 PC 的孩子，改变了他的人生轨迹。我想知道还有多少人有着和我类似的故事。

我一直记着这句话：

“那些疯狂到以为自己能改变世界的人，才是真正改变世界的人。”

还有那么多值得做的东西。可以倾注心血的东西。能让某个人感受到我第一次看到那台 iMac 时感受的东西。

谢谢你，Steve。💛
