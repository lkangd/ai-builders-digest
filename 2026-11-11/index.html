If you are a product leader, here is a simple exercise: draw out your product lifecycle.

e.g. market understanding -> design -> development -> GTM.

Chances are your PMs spend more time in some stages than others - because of the nature of your product, your org or their comfort zones.

This is a handoff-heavy, lossy process.

Now ask yourself: using AI, how much deeper can PMs go on every stage?

PMs should be able to
- build a much deeper, broader understanding of users and the market
- iterate fast from ideas to working prototypes
- explore the codebase to study technical constraints and tradeoffs
- run their own analytics and investigate questions as they emerge
- understand the GTM funnel deeply enough to shape product strategy

Now, this is not about PMs replacing every role. As the function that spans the entire lifecycle, this is about raising the bar on the PM role, so they combine their breadth with much greater depth.
译文
如果你是产品负责人，有个简单练习：画出你的产品生命周期。

例如：市场理解 -> 设计 -> 开发 -> GTM。

很可能你的 PM 在某些阶段投入的时间比其他阶段更多——因为产品性质、组织架构或他们的舒适区。

这是一个交接繁重、信息损耗大的流程。

现在问自己：借助 AI，PM 在每个阶段能深入多少？

PM 应该能够：
- 对用户和市场建立更深入、更广泛的理解
- 从想法快速迭代到可运行原型
- 探索代码库，研究技术约束与权衡
- 自己跑分析，并在问题出现时深入调查
- 深入理解 GTM 漏斗，从而塑造产品策略

这不是让 PM 取代每个角色。作为贯穿整个生命周期的职能，这是要提高 PM 角色的标准，让他们把广度与更大的深度结合起来。
