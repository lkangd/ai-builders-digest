My “armchair” thoughts on the personal agent race:

1. Muse is poised to become the leader. The app is intuitive, and Meta is aggressively promoting it across all its properties. Once you use the Muse app, you realize that it makes little sense for an agent to live in an existing messaging app like iMessage. Unless Meta runs out of compute or something, I think this will become the company’s most successful homegrown app after Facebook. It will be much bigger than Threads.

2. ChatGPT is probably still the personal agent leader because it already has 1B+ users. OpenAI’s models, computer use, and voice are arguably better than Muse feature by feature. But it’s hard to build one product for both work and personal use, and harder still to serve enterprises and consumers with the same UX. Even for work, I started using Grok Bot because the Work/Codex split remains too confusing. I’m sure the team is cooking, and we’ll see something good in the next few weeks.

3. Grok Bot isn’t really a Muse competitor because it’s focused on work. Multiple bots naturally fit team-based knowledge work IMO. My bull case for Grok Bot is that it's a few features away from becoming multiplayer agentic Slack for both bots and humans.

4. Google is obviously the dark horse candidate. All these personal agents live off Gmail, Google Calendar, and your Google app's data. Google has their own personal agent in Spark but the last I checked it's a secondary tab in Gemini. I think Google needs to be far more aggressive in making Spark the primary experience and making it intuitive and competitive with Muse. Google however doesn't have a great track record in multiplayer AI...

5. Speaking of multiplayer, nobody has figured this out yet. I can’t easily add my spouse to a Muse chat to plan a vacation. I also can’t loop coworkers into ChatGPT or Grok Bot threads. Multiplayer AI today mostly means tagging bots in Slack. Every major provider is probably working on this, and I think it will be the next big unlock.

My personal AI stack right now is Muse for personal, Grok Bot for cloud tasks, Claude for specific use cases, and ChatGPT for everything else.

But I think we’re going to see some major updates this week.

Everyone should design their personal skills and files so they’re easy to port between harnesses and agents. Otherwise, you’ll spend half your time moving from one tool to another.

我对个人智能体竞赛的“纸上谈兵”看法：

1. Muse 有望成为领导者。这款应用直观易用，Meta 正在所有旗下平台大力推广。一旦你用上 Muse 应用，就会意识到智能体活在 iMessage 这类现有消息应用里没什么意义。除非 Meta 算力耗尽之类的，否则我认为它将成为该公司继 Facebook 之后最成功的自研应用。它会比 Threads 大得多。

2. ChatGPT 可能仍是个人智能体领导者，因为它已经有 10 亿+ 用户。OpenAI 的模型、computer use 和语音，逐项对比可以说都优于 Muse。但很难为工作和个人用途打造同一款产品，用同样的 UX 服务企业和消费者就更难了。即便为了工作，我也开始用 Grok Bot 了，因为 Work/Codex 的拆分仍然太令人困惑。我相信团队正在憋大招，接下来几周我们就会看到好东西。

3. Grok Bot 其实算不上 Muse 的竞品，因为它聚焦工作。在我看来，多个 bot 天然适合团队知识工作。我看好 Grok Bot 的理由是，它距离成为面向 bot 和人类的多玩家智能体版 Slack 只差几个功能。

4. Google 显然是黑马候选。所有这些个人智能体都靠 Gmail、Google Calendar 和 Google 应用的数据活着。Google 在 Spark 里有自己的个人智能体，但我上次查看时，它只是 Gemini 里的一个次要标签页。我认为 Google 需要激进得多，把 Spark 做成主要体验，并让它直观易用、能与 Muse 竞争。不过 Google 在多玩家 AI 上的过往记录不佳……

5. 说到多玩家，还没人搞定这个。我没法轻松把配偶加进 Muse 聊天来规划假期。我也没法把同事拉进 ChatGPT 或 Grok Bot 的会话。如今的多玩家 AI 基本就是在 Slack 里 @bot。每个主要玩家大概都在做这个，我认为这会是下一个大突破。

我目前的个人 AI 栈是：个人用 Muse，云任务用 Grok Bot，特定用例用 Claude，其他一切用 ChatGPT。

但我认为这周我们会看到一些重大更新。

每个人都应该把自己的个人技能和文件设计成便于在不同 harness 和智能体之间迁移。否则，你一半时间都会花在从一个工具搬到另一个工具上。
