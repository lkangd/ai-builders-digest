This year we’ve had a clear jump in model capability where instead of telling agents precisely what to do, it’s better to use these systems to solve problems you can’t easily define yourself.

A year ago you’d need to very clearly tell agents the how to design and develop your product idea, or there was almost no chance of success. Now, increasingly, the agent likely can come up with a better implementation than you’d have thought of because you can have it connect the dots between a far greater solution space than you can.

This will be increasingly true in basically every domain of kneeler work. We’re going to see this impact in finance, legal, life sciences, cyber, and many other spaces where no person can hold onto all the available relevant industry knowledge to think of the best solution.

Ultimately this is going to reshape our relationship with AI and how we deploy agents in our work. It won’t be about explicitly telling them, but instead giving them goals and enough freedom to pursue them in the best way they can.
译文
今年我们看到模型能力出现了明显跃升：与其精确告诉 agent 该做什么，不如用这些系统去解决你自己难以清楚定义的问题。

一年前，你必须非常明确地告诉 agent 该如何设计和开发你的产品想法，否则几乎没有成功的可能。而现在，agent 越来越有可能想出比你预想更好的实现，因为它能在远超你所能把握的方案空间里把点连起来。

这将越来越适用于几乎每一个知识工作领域。我们会在金融、法律、生命科学、cyber 以及许多其他领域看到这种影响，因为没有人能掌握所有相关的行业知识，从而想出最佳方案。

最终，这将重塑我们与 AI 的关系，以及我们在工作中部署 agent 的方式。重点将不再是明确告诉它们怎么做，而是给它们目标，并给予足够的自由度，让它们以最好的方式去追求这些目标。

Most of us are managing agents all wrong

We setup agents to do things we already know how to do, then tell them exactly how we want them to do things

This is treating agents like interns

That was fine earlier this year when models were not as capable

But ask yourself - if you just hired a 130 IQ employee, would you just have them do things you already know how to do and tell them exactly how to do it?

If you’re using frontier models with agents, you want to give them goals and objectives for things you don’t know how to do, then empower them to do the things

Hire agents as experts, not interns

Yes you should still have some agent interns to automate boring work to free up more of your time

But if you’re not using agents to do things you don’t know how to do then you’re missing out

Let them go figure out how to do the things that expand beyond your skills and capabilities just like a good leader would when hiring a team
