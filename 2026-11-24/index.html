Yes to all of this. As agents take on more and more long running project work, and you have swarms of agents that parallelize work against a goal, the agent harness and architecture for orchestration will have to evolve.

One of the biggest shifts is how those agents collectively share their knowledge, keep track of the tasks, and also coordinate with human users. This in theory is technically straightforward, but there’s a ton of complexity to get right to ensure agents can both get access to the revenant resources without too much or little exposure.

There are a lot of lessons to pull from based on how people already do this kind of work together (people share their files, have computers to work with, community in chats and inboxes, and so on). But equally the primitives need to be able to enable this work at a throughput far higher than people, and in a world where agents can much more easily become a vector of security risk.

Going to be super fun building out this next generation of how agents operate. Lots of opportunity here both for horizontal and vertical specific approaches to bring this to life.
译文
是的，这些我都同意。随着 agents 承担越来越多的长期项目工作，并且你拥有一群 agents 针对一个目标并行推进，agent harness 和编排架构也必须演进。

最大的变化之一，是这些 agents 如何集体共享知识、跟踪任务，以及如何与人类用户协调。这在理论上技术上很直接，但要做对会有大量复杂性，既要确保 agents 能获取到相关资源，又不能暴露过多或过少。

从人们已经如何一起做这类工作中，可以吸取很多经验（人们会共享文件、用电脑工作、在聊天和收件箱里交流，等等）。但同样，这些原语必须能支撑远高于人类的吞吐，而且是在 agents 更容易成为安全风险来源的世界里。
