People overcomplicate venture investing - it's deceptively simple..

1) You have to win the right to
2) Find and invest in exceptional founders
3) Just before it's "consensus"
4) Consistently every year

Vast majority of VCs are able to do maybe one of these right, some are able to do a few but to be truly truly great, you genuinely have to do ALL of them right.

And, that consistency of being right is REALLY hard and less talked about. You have to really want it and see it - Djokovic style, enjoying hitting the ball everyday and always focused on the next point to be won.

Regardless, of what your track record is, or how much wealth you have accumulated. Or how many misses you have had. Or, what the TVPI is of the current (or last) fund. Or, which star GP left your firm.

Because the next opportunity might be around the corner, and it might go to someone else, if you didn't want it enough.

And, sure the naysayers might say the feedback loop is long, you can "fake" it for a while especially if you have an early hit (power law etc) but it does catch up. In at least how your mindset is, how you meet founders and your own ability to capture the next wave.
译文
人们把风险投资想得太复杂——它其实简单得具有欺骗性……

1）你得先赢得资格，才能
2）找到并投资卓越创始人
3）赶在它成为“共识”之前
4）每年持续做到

绝大多数 VC 也许只能做对其中一件，有些能做对几件，但要真正真正伟大，你必须把每一件都做对。

而且，持续做对真的非常难，也少有人谈。你必须真心渴望并看见它——像 Djokovic 那样，享受每天击球，始终专注于下一个要赢的得分。

不管你的业绩记录如何，积累了多少财富；错过了多少次；当前（或上一支）基金的 TVPI 是多少；或哪位明星 GP 离开了你的公司。

因为下一个机会可能就在转角，而如果你不够想要它，它可能就归了别人。

当然，唱反调的人会说反馈周期很长，你可以“装”一阵子，尤其如果你早期就投中过（幂律等），但它终究会追上你。至少会体现在你的心态、你见创始人的方式，以及你自己抓住下一波浪潮的能力上。
